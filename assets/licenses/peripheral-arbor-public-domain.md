# Paul Spooner / Peripheral Arbor public-domain dedication

Source: https://ip.tryop.com/
Retrieved: 2026-10-31

The creator identifies his work as public domain and permits unrestricted use,
adaptation, and transformation of his past and future work. The dedication is
dated 2014-08-05. Each of the three original Sketchfab model descriptions
explicitly links to this policy and states that its actual license is public
domain. The exact model metadata is retained in the adjacent JSON files.

The Sketchfab structured license field also lists CC BY 4.0. To satisfy that
route as well, the app's About panel and ASSET_LICENSES.md retain author,
source, license, and modification credits.
